JustBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

JustBot ("the Bot") is a Discord bot that answers questions using an AI model. This policy explains what information the Bot handles.

What the Bot receives

The Bot only reads messages that are sent directly to it: messages that @mention the Bot, direct messages to the Bot, and messages sent with its slash commands (such as /ask). It does not act on other messages in a server. When you talk to it, the Bot receives the text of your message, your Discord display name, and the ID of the channel the message was sent in.

How the information is used

Your message and display name are sent to the Anthropic API so an AI model can write a reply. The Bot also keeps the most recent messages from each channel in the computer's memory so it can follow the conversation. This memory is temporary: it is not saved to a database or a file, it is erased whenever the Bot restarts, and anyone can clear it for their channel with the /reset command.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or build user profiles. We do not store message history permanently. We do not collect passwords, payment details, or any information outside of Discord.

Third parties

Messages sent to the Bot are processed by Anthropic to generate replies, and are subject to Anthropic's privacy policy. The Bot runs on Discord and is subject to Discord's privacy policy. Please do not share sensitive personal information with the Bot.

Data deletion

Because conversation memory is temporary, there is nothing stored long term to delete. You can use /reset to clear a channel's memory, or contact us below if you have any questions or requests.

Children

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
